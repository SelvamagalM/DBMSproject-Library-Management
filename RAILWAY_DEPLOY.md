# Railway Deployment Guide

## Why Railway instead of Vercel?
- ✅ **Easier** - No serverless configuration needed
- ✅ **Works with Flask** - Simple full-stack deployment
- ✅ **Free tier** - $5/month free credit
- ✅ **Persistent storage** - Can store SQLite database

## Deploy to Railway in 2 minutes:

1. **Go to:** https://railway.app
2. **Click "Start a New Project"**
3. **Select "Deploy from GitHub"**
4. **Authorize Railway with GitHub**
5. **Select repository:** `DBMSproject-Library-Management`
6. **Select branch:** `master`
7. **Click "Deploy Now"**
8. **Wait 2-3 minutes** for deployment
9. **Get your public URL** from the dashboard

## That's it! Your app will be live immediately.

Railway handles everything automatically - no configuration needed!
